The Salvation Lottery

If salvation depends on believing the right message, how much should birthplace, family, and access to that message affect a person’s eternal fate?

The test is not “all religions are true.” It asks whether a perfectly fair judgment can depend on a condition people encounter under wildly unequal circumstances.
Nothing you choose here is sent anywhere, saved, or graded. Your answers stay in this browser tab while you take the test.

Before we begin

There is no score and no trick. Pick the answer closest to what you really believe. When there is a strong Christian reply, we will show it.

You can go back and change any answer.
