The Original Sin Test

If Adam sinned, what exactly passes to people born thousands of years later: guilt, consequences, both, or neither?

There is a big difference between inheriting damage from someone else’s act and being morally guilty for that act.
Nothing you choose here is sent anywhere, saved, or graded. Your answers stay in this browser tab while you take the test.

Before we begin

There is no score and no trick. Pick the answer closest to what you really believe. When there is a strong Christian reply, we will show it.

You can go back and change any answer.
