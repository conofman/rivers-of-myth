Bible Tests

Ten guided tests of common Christian bedrock claims. No scores. No forced conclusion. You choose what you believe, and the test follows the answer.

The goal is not to make a weak version of Christianity easy to knock down. Each test gives strong Christian replies a real branch — and shows what that reply solves, what it changes, and what question remains.

The rule for every test

Plain language. One hard idea at a time. No “gotcha” if a real answer works. If a defense solves the problem by changing an original claim, the result says so. If the defense really does answer the challenge, the result says that too.
