The Hell Test

Christians disagree about hell. This test asks what each view has to explain — especially if the punishment is conscious and never ends.

The question is not whether wrongdoing matters. It is whether the punishment fits the crime, the choice, and the opportunity the person actually had.
Nothing you choose here is sent anywhere, saved, or graded. Your answers stay in this browser tab while you take the test.

Before we begin

There is no score and no trick. Pick the answer closest to what you really believe. When there is a strong Christian reply, we will show it.

You can go back and change any answer.
