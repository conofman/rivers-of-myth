The Moral Authority Test

Is something good because God says so — or does God say it because it is good? There is a third Christian answer too: God’s nature is goodness itself. Let’s test all three.

The point is not to prove morality can exist without God. It is to ask what the sentence “God is good” actually means.
Nothing you choose here is sent anywhere, saved, or graded. Your answers stay in this browser tab while you take the test.

Before we begin

There is no score and no trick. Pick the answer closest to what you really believe. When there is a strong Christian reply, we will show it.

You can go back and change any answer.
