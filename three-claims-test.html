The Three Claims Test

Christians often describe God with three big claims: God can do anything that is possible, God knows everything, and God is perfectly good. But there is one more fact that matters: God is also said to have created the world.

So the question is not only why God allows suffering now. Why create a world with suffering in it at all?
Nothing you choose here is sent anywhere, saved, or graded. Your answers stay in this browser tab while you take the test.

Before we begin

There is no score and no trick. Pick the answer closest to what you really believe. When there is a strong Christian reply, we will show it.
